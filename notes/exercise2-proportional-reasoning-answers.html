1

Juice mixture

Water : syrup : lemon juice = 3 : 2 : 1. Water = 12 L.

3 parts = 12 L, so 1 part = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 L.

Syrup = 2 × 4 = 8 L; lemon juice = 1 × 4 = 4 L.

Answer: Syrup = 8 L; total mixture = 24 L.

2

Divide ₹2400

Ratio = 5 : 3 : 2.

Total parts = 5 + 3 + 2 = 10.

One part = ₹2400 ÷ 10 = ₹240.

Answer: ₹1200, ₹720 and ₹480.

3

Mixture in ratio 3 : 2 : 1

Total mixture = 60 L.

Total parts = 3 + 2 + 1 = 6; one part = 60 ÷ 6 = 10 L.

Answer: 30 L, 20 L and 10 L.

Multiply each ratio term by 120/7.

Workers and days

12 workers finish in 10 days. How many days for 15 workers?

Workers × days = constant.

12 × 10 = 15 × x, so x = 120 ÷ 15.

Answer: 8 days.

6

Pipes filling a tank

3 pipes fill it in 16 hours. Time with 6 pipes?

3 × 16 = 6 × x, so x = 48 ÷ 6.

Answer: 8 hours.

7

Food for students

Food lasts 50 students for 12 days. If 25 more join, how long?

New total = 50 + 25 = 75 students.

50 × 12 = 75 × x, so x = 600 ÷ 75.

Answer: 8 days.

8

Travel time and speed

6 hours at 50 km/h; new speed = 75 km/h.

Distance = 50 × 6 = 300 km.

Time = 300 ÷ 75 = 4 hours.

Answer: 4 hours.

9

Machines and days

20 machines make the goods in 9 days. How many machines for 6 days?

Machines × days = constant.

20 × 9 = x × 6, so x = 180 ÷ 6.

Answer: 30 machines.

10

Workers painting a wall

4 workers take 6 days. How long for 12 workers?

4 × 6 = 12 × x, so x = 24 ÷ 12.

Answer: 2 days.

11

Complete work together

A completes in 4 hours, B in 6 hours.

A's rate = 1/4 work/hour; B's rate = 1/6.

Together = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12 work/hour.

Time = 1 ÷ (5/12) = 12/5 hours.

Answer: 2.4 hours = 2 hours 24 minutes.

12

Food supply after an epidemic

150 workers have food for 120 days. After 20 days, 50 workers leave.

Food remaining = 150 × (120 − 20) = 15,000 worker-days.

Remaining workers = 150 − 50 = 100.

Remaining days = 15,000 ÷ 100 = 150 days.

Answer: 150 more days.

13

Machines and production

30 machines produce goods in 50 days. Time for 60 machines?

30 × 50 = 60 × x, so x = 1500 ÷ 60.

Answer: 25 days.

14

Car speed

3 hours at 40 km/h; new speed = 60 km/h.

Distance = 3 × 40 = 120 km.

Time = 120 ÷ 60 = 2 hours.

Answer: 2 hours.

15

School periods

5 periods last 50 minutes in total; 10 periods in a day.

Duration of one period = 50 ÷ 5 = 10 minutes.

Answer: 10 minutes per period.

16

Two pipes fill a tank

One pipe fills in 4 hours and the other in 6 hours.

Combined rate = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12 tank/hour.

Time = 1 ÷ (5/12) = 12/5 hours.

Answer: 2 hours 24 minutes.

Transport pie chart

Bus 120°, Walk 90°, Bicycle 60°, Car 60°, Auto 30°.

(i) Largest sector is Bus (120°).

(ii) Car fraction = 60°/360° = 1/6.

(iii) Bicycle share = 60/360 = 1/6; 20 students represent 1/6, so total = 20 × 6.

(iv) Equal-angle modes are Bicycle and Car (both 60°).

Answer: (i) Bus (ii) 1/6 (iii) 120 students (iv) Bicycle and Car.

Library books in a ratio

23

Which are inverse proportions?

Decide which situations have one quantity increasing while the other decreases proportionally.

(i) Workers and time to finish a fixed job: inverse.

(ii) Time and distance at uniform speed: direct, not inverse.

(iii) Area of land and crop harvested: generally direct, not inverse.

(iv) Time and speed for a fixed journey: inverse.

(v) Population and area of land per person (fixed land area): inverse.

Answer: (i), (iv) and (v).

24

Bottles and boxes

25 boxes hold 12 bottles each. Repack 20 bottles per box.

Total bottles = 25 × 12 = 300.

Boxes needed = 300 ÷ 20 = 15.

Answer: 15 boxes.

25

People fitting windows

Two persons fit 2 windows in 3 days. How long for one person? How many people for 2 windows in one day?

Work = 2 persons × 3 days = 6 person-days for 2 windows.

(i) One person needs 6 days for the same work.

(ii) To finish in 1 day, people needed = 6 ÷ 1 = 6.

Answer: (i) 6 days (ii) 6 persons.
